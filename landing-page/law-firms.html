What we automate

Built for the way law firms actually operate

Every automation is custom-built around your practice areas, existing software, and how your team works. No generic templates.

⚡

New client intake

Contact form submission triggers an automatic SMS or email follow-up within minutes. An AI agent qualifies the enquiry — practice area, urgency, context — and books the initial consultation into your calendar with a brief attached. No lead goes cold waiting for someone on your team.

✅

Conflict check workflow

New enquiry automatically sends a structured conflict check questionnaire to the prospective client. Responses are collected and surfaced to the relevant partner before any time is committed. The manual back-and-forth is eliminated.

📋

Document collection

Engagement letters, signed authorities, ID verification, supporting documents — automatically requested and chased until received. Your team gets notified when a client's file is complete and ready to action. No manual follow-up required.

💬

Matter update communications

Clients waiting for updates are a constant drain on paralegal time. Automated milestone notifications keep clients informed at defined stages in a matter — without anyone on your team drafting or sending individual updates.

💰

Invoice & payment follow-up

Overdue invoices trigger an automated reminder sequence — polite, firm, and timed appropriately. Stops automatically when payment is received. Eliminates the awkward manual chase from fee earners.

📅

Deadline & key date alerts

Court dates, limitation periods, filing deadlines — automated internal alerts ensure the right people are notified at the right time. Client-facing reminders for their required actions are sent automatically alongside.
