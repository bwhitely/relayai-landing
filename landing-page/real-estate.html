What we automate

Built for sales and property management teams

From the first after-hours enquiry to lease renewal — every repetitive touchpoint in the property lifecycle can run automatically.

🌙

After-hours enquiry & inspection booking

Property enquiries don't wait until 9am. An AI agent responds immediately to after-hours enquiries, answers common questions about the property, and offers available inspection times — booking confirmed before your team starts the day.

🔔

Inspection reminders

Every registered attendee receives automated reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before an inspection. Significantly reduces no-shows. Attendees can confirm or cancel — responses update your list automatically.

📥

Tenant application document collection

Application submitted — automated sequence requests the specific documents needed: ID, payslips, rental ledger, references. Applicants are nudged automatically for outstanding items until the file is complete. Your team is notified when it's ready to assess.

📄

Lease renewal workflows

Upcoming lease renewals are tracked automatically. Tenants receive an automated outreach the right number of weeks before expiry — intent to renew, proposed terms, next steps. No renewal falls through because it slipped off the PM's radar.

🔧

Maintenance request routing

Tenant submits a maintenance request — automatically categorised by urgency and trade, routed to the appropriate contractor, and a confirmation sent to the tenant. Updates flow back when the job is scheduled and completed. No email chains, no dropped requests.

💰

Rental arrears follow-up

Rent overdue — an automated, professional reminder goes to the tenant with escalating follow-ups timed to your arrears policy. Stops when payment is received or the matter is escalated. Frees your PMs from the uncomfortable manual chase entirely.
