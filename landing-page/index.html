Wait, what's an agent?

Think of it as a digital employee.

An agent is a piece of software that can read, understand, and respond — just like a person would. It can hold a conversation with a customer over text, pull up information from your systems, fill in a form, send a follow-up, or flag something for your attention.

The difference? It never clocks off. It doesn't forget steps. It doesn't need training twice. And it works inside the tools you already use — your CRM, your email, your spreadsheets — so nothing changes about how you run your business.

You don't need to understand how it works under the hood. You just tell us what you need done, and we build an agent that does it.

What It Does

Small business automation that actually fits.

Every agent is built to fit your business. Here are some of the things they can do.

Handle Customer Enquiries

Respond to questions via WhatsApp, SMS, or web chat instantly — with accurate, context-aware answers about your services, pricing, and availability.

Your agent collects the details you need from new enquiries and saves them straight to your CRM. By the time you call back, you already know the job.

Automate Admin Work

Follow-ups, appointment reminders, data entry, invoice chasing — the tasks that eat your day but don't need your expertise. Gone.

Connect Your Tools

Your agent plugs into the systems you already use — CRMs, spreadsheets, booking platforms, email. No ripping and replacing, just smarter workflows.

Escalate When It Matters

Not everything should be automated. Your agent knows when to loop in a human and sends you a summary with all the context you need to take over.
