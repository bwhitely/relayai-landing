What kind of automation?

Not everything needs AI. Sometimes a simple automation saves more time.

Simple: Connecting your apps — a form submission creates a CRM record, an invoice triggers a follow-up email, a new booking updates your calendar. No AI needed, just wiring things together.

Medium: Multi-step workflow automation — conditional logic, approvals, data routing across systems. The kind of processes you currently do manually because "that's how it's always been done."

Advanced: AI agents that understand context, hold conversations, and act autonomously — handling customer enquiries, booking appointments, escalating when needed.

We figure out which level your problem needs and build the right solution. No overengineering, no unnecessary complexity.

What It Does

Automation built around how your business actually runs.

Every solution is designed specifically for your workflows. Here's what that can look like.

Automate Enquiry Handling

AI agents or auto-responders that handle incoming enquiries instantly — across WhatsApp, SMS, email, or web chat — so nothing slips through.

Connect Your Systems

Sync your CRM, calendar, accounting, and comms tools so data flows between them automatically instead of being copied by hand.

E.g., a new Xero invoice automatically creates a follow-up task in HubSpot and updates the client's record in your spreadsheet.

Eliminate Manual Data Entry

Auto-populate forms, records, and reports from the data you already have — no more re-typing the same information across three different systems.

E.g., a completed Calendly booking flows straight into your CRM, calendar, and accounting system — no copy-paste, no lag.

Streamline Repetitive Workflows

Turn multi-step manual processes into automated flows — approvals, follow-ups, notifications, and handoffs that run without you.

E.g., an overdue invoice triggers a reminder email, a Slack alert to the owner, and a CRM status update — all automatically.

Keep Humans in Control

Escalation, approvals, and dashboards so you always know what's happening and can step in when a human touch is needed.

E.g., complex enquiries are flagged to your team via Slack with full conversation context — no dropped balls, no missed handoffs.

Allied Health Automation

Appointment reminders, rebooking flows, NDIS document routing, and overdue invoice follow-up — purpose-built for Splose-based practices.

E.g., SMS reminders sent 24h before each appointment. No-shows trigger an automatic rebooking prompt. Overdue invoices chase themselves.
