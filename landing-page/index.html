Wait, what's an agent?

Think of it as a digital employee.

An agent is a piece of software that can read, understand, and respond — just like a person would. It can hold a conversation with a customer over text, pull up information from your systems, fill in a form, send a follow-up, or flag something for your attention.

The difference? It never clocks off. It doesn't forget steps. It doesn't need training twice. And it works inside the tools you already use — your CRM, your email, your spreadsheets — so nothing changes about how you run your business.

You don't need to understand how it works under the hood. You just tell us what you need done, and we build an agent that does it.
