Business Automation Adelaide: Custom Automation for Local SMBs

20 February 2026|6 min read

Adelaide's economy is dominated by small and medium businesses — allied health clinics, trades operators, professional services firms, retailers, and hospitality venues. These businesses generate most of the city's employment and do most of the actual economic work. They are also, almost without exception, understaffed for the administrative load they carry.

The tools they use do not talk to each other. A booking system that does not connect to the CRM. An accounting platform that has no visibility into the calendar. A WhatsApp number that takes messages but requires a human to check it and type back. The gap between these systems is filled by manual work — copying, checking, chasing, entering. Hours every week, per person, on tasks that follow a predictable pattern every single time.

RelayAI is an Adelaide-based automation consultancy. We build custom automations that connect the tools Adelaide businesses already use — so the repetitive work handles itself, without disrupting how the business operates and without requiring anyone to migrate to a new platform or learn new software.

What we actually build

We do not sell a platform. We build custom automations, specific to each client's tools, workflows, and pain points. What that looks like in practice varies by business, but the work falls into a handful of categories:

Workflow automations. When something happens, something else should happen automatically. A new enquiry arrives, a record gets created, a confirmation is sent, a task is assigned. When an invoice is paid, a follow-up email goes out and the CRM is updated. When a staff member marks a job complete, the client gets notified. These are rule-based chains of events that currently require a person to do them manually — and that person's time is the cost you are paying every day they do it.

System integrations. Most Adelaide businesses are running three to six separate tools, none of which talk to each other out of the box. We connect them. A new patient in Cliniko appears automatically in Google Calendar. A won deal in HubSpot creates a draft invoice in Xero. A job booked in ServiceM8 fires an appointment reminder via SMS. The tools stay the same; the manual transfer between them disappears.

Document processing. Invoices, referral forms, intake paperwork, purchase orders — documents that arrive and need to be read, interpreted, and entered somewhere. We automate the capture and extraction step: the document comes in, the relevant fields are pulled out, and the data lands where it needs to go without anyone reading it by hand.

AI agents. Automated systems that handle inbound enquiries via WhatsApp, SMS, web chat, or email. The agent understands what the customer is asking, checks availability, answers common questions, books appointments, and escalates to a human when the situation calls for it. This is not a chatbot that replies with a FAQ list — it is a system that can actually take action.

Automated reporting. Weekly summaries, monthly performance reports, lead pipeline snapshots — generated and delivered automatically to whoever needs them. No one needs to pull the data, build a spreadsheet, and send an email. It happens on schedule, every time.

We work with whatever tools the client already has. Xero, MYOB, HubSpot, Google Workspace, Splose, Cliniko, Calendly, Nookal, ServiceM8, Shopify — if the tool has an API or a webhook, we can connect it. We are not going to ask you to switch platforms in order to work with us.

Industries we work with in Adelaide

Automation looks different in every industry, because the workflows, tools, and bottlenecks are different. Here is what it tends to look like for the sectors we work with most in Adelaide.

Allied health. Adelaide has a large allied health sector — physiotherapy, psychology, occupational therapy, speech pathology, dietetics — spread across the city fringe, the Hills, and the northern suburbs. These practices run on appointment-heavy workflows where admin burden is disproportionate to practice size. Patient intake, appointment reminders, recall campaigns, referral handling, gap payment follow-up — most of it is repetitive and rule-based. We integrate with Splose and Cliniko directly, so automations connect to the system the practice is already using rather than sitting alongside it.

Trades. Plumbers, electricians, builders, landscapers, and HVAC contractors in Adelaide face a specific problem: they are in the field all day, they cannot answer the phone, and enquiries that do not get a quick response go to the next tradie on the list. After-hours enquiry handling, quote follow-up sequences, job scheduling notifications, and invoice reminders are all automatable. The result is fewer lost jobs and less time spent on administrative catch-up at the end of the day.

Professional services. Accountants, financial planners, lawyers, and management consultants deal with client onboarding paperwork, document collection, and the ongoing back-and-forth of getting what they need from clients before they can do the actual work. Automating the request, reminder, and acknowledgement steps in that process frees up senior staff time for billable work instead of administrative follow-up.

Retail and hospitality. Stock management triggers, supplier reorder notifications, staff scheduling communications, post-visit customer follow-up, loyalty programme touchpoints. The volume of operational detail in retail and hospitality is high, the margin is thin, and the cost of missed tasks is immediate. Automation does not replace the human judgment in these businesses — it handles the structured, repeating tasks so the humans can focus on service.

What automation looks like for an Adelaide business

Here is a concrete example, because abstract descriptions of automation do not make it easy to picture what actually changes.

A physiotherapy clinic in the Adelaide Hills closes at 6pm. At 8:17pm on a Tuesday, a prospective patient sends a WhatsApp message: "Hi, do you have any appointments available this week? I've hurt my shoulder."

Without automation: the message sits in the WhatsApp inbox overnight. The receptionist sees it the next morning, checks the calendar manually, types a reply, and sends it at 9:05am. By that point, the patient has already booked with a clinic that responded at 8:30pm the night before.

With automation: the patient gets a reply within thirty seconds. The system asks a short intake question about the nature of the injury. It checks available appointment slots in real time against the clinic's Cliniko calendar. It offers the patient two or three options. The patient selects one. The appointment is booked, the patient record is created or updated, a confirmation message is sent with the clinic address and a reminder set for 24 hours before the appointment. The physiotherapist arrives the next morning and the appointment is already in the calendar, the patient's details are already in the system, and no one at the clinic did anything.

That is not a hypothetical. That is what we build. The same pattern applies to a plumber getting a quote request at 9pm, a psychologist receiving a referral on a Friday afternoon, or a retailer's supplier sending a stock confirmation on a Saturday. The after-hours response gap is one of the most consistent sources of lost business for Adelaide SMBs, and it is one of the most straightforward things to close with automation.

How we work

The process is straightforward and does not require a long commitment before you see results.

Discovery. We start by mapping the process you want to automate. What triggers it? What are the steps? Where does the data go? Which tools are involved? This is a practical conversation, not a consulting exercise — we want to understand the exact workflow so we can build something that fits it precisely.

Build. We build the automation around your existing tools. No migrations, no new platforms for your team to learn. The build happens on our side. You keep using what you use; you just stop having to do the manual steps in between.

Test and go live. We test thoroughly before anything touches your live data or your customers. Most builds take two to four weeks depending on complexity. We walk through the automation with you before it goes live so you understand exactly what it does and when.

Ongoing support. A monthly hosting and maintenance fee covers keeping the automation running, handling any changes to your tools or workflows, and fixing anything that breaks. Integrations break when APIs change or tools update — that is on us to handle, not on you to figure out.

Most projects are running and delivering measurable value within a month of the initial conversation.
