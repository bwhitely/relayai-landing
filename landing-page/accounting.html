What we automate

Built for accounting and advisory practices

From tax document collection to Xero-connected reporting, every automation is custom-built around your workflows. No off-the-shelf templates.

⚡

New client qualification

A contact form enquiry triggers an immediate AI-powered follow-up via SMS or email. The AI qualifies the prospect — type of work, current situation, urgency — and books a discovery meeting with the right person on your team. Whoever responds first wins the engagement.

📥

Tax document collection

When it's time to file, clients receive an automated sequence requesting their specific outstanding items. Follow-up reminders stop the moment they upload. Your team gets notified when a file is complete. No manual chasing, no spreadsheet tracking.

🤝

Client onboarding

New engagement triggers a structured information collection workflow — entity details, director information, existing software access, signed engagement letter. Everything collected and filed before the first billable hour is spent.

📊

Xero-connected reporting

Scheduled management reports pulled directly from client Xero files, formatted, and delivered automatically on a set schedule. Your team reviews and advises — not compiles. Works across your entire client book simultaneously.

🔔

Internal follow-up sequences

Outstanding documents, pending approvals, items needing a client nudge before a deadline — automated sequences handle the chasing. Triggered by your conditions, stopped automatically when the client responds. Your team focuses on the work that requires them.

📅

Compliance calendar alerts

Annual lodgement deadlines, review dates, ATO obligations — automated internal alerts when deadlines approach and client documents aren't in yet. Client-facing reminders sent alongside so nothing slips through.
