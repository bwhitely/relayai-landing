# Discovery Interview Questions

Use these during the initial discovery call with a prospective client. Take notes directly in this document or copy to a client-specific file.

---

## About the Business

1. What's the name of your business, and what do you do?
2. Where are you located? Do you serve a specific area?
3. How many staff do you have?
4. What services do you offer, and what are the rough price ranges?
5. What kind of work are you most looking to automate — is it customer-facing stuff like handling enquiries and bookings,       internal processes like data entry and follow-ups,  
  or connecting systems so information flows between them automatically?

## Current Situation

6. How do customers currently reach you? (phone, email, web form, Facebook, WhatsApp, walk-in)
7. Roughly how many enquiries do you get per week?
8. Who handles those enquiries today? How much of their time does it take?
9. What happens to enquiries that come in after hours or when you're busy?
10. Do you have any idea how many enquiries you miss or lose?

## Pain Points

11. What's the single biggest headache with manual or repetitive work in your business?
12. Are there repetitive questions you get asked all the time? (pricing, availability, location, "do you do X?")
13. Have you ever lost a customer because you didn't get back to them fast enough?
14. Is there any admin work you'd love to never do again?

## Workflows & Processes

15. Walk me through a typical day — what are the most time-consuming manual tasks?
16. Are there processes where you're copying data between systems? (e.g. form → CRM, invoice → spreadsheet)
17. Do you have multi-step processes that follow predictable rules? (approvals, follow-ups, handoffs)
18. Are there things that fall through the cracks because they depend on someone remembering to do them?

## Systems & Tools

19. Do you use a CRM? If so, which one? (HubSpot, Salesforce, spreadsheet, nothing)
20. Do you use a booking/scheduling system? Which one? (Splose, Cliniko, Calendly, manual)
21. Do you have a website? What platform is it on? (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, custom)
22. Do you use WhatsApp for business at all?

## Desired Outcome

23. If you had a perfect assistant handling enquiries, what would they do?
24. What information should the assistant collect from every new enquiry? (name, phone, email, what they need)
25. When should the assistant hand off to a human? (complex questions, complaints, bookings)
26. Is there anything the assistant should never say or promise?
27. Beyond enquiry handling, are there other manual workflows you'd love to automate?
28. If we could connect any two systems in your business, which would save you the most time?

## Budget & Timeline

29. Have you looked into AI or automation tools before? What happened?
30. What's your budget for something like this?
31. When would you ideally want this up and running?
32. Who would be involved in the decision? Is it just you, or do other people need to sign off?
33. How will you enable me to test and implement the work? Am I able to be granted access to software systems, or provided documents to test my automation creations against?

Final statement: Alright, thanks for all of that information. What I'll do now is do some research and try to come up with a plan to help your business with automation. Note that this is all free, and we'll begin the pricing discussion and terms once I have presented a solution to you, which will include the pricing terms.
